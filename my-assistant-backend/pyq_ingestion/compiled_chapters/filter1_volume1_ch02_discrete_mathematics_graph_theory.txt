topics in computer science.

Visual Deception:

Check Invariants Systematically:

Match Vertices by Properties:

unique cycle structure) and try to map them.
Adjacency Matrix Comparison:
 If two graphs are isomorphic, their adjacency matrices can be made identical
by permuting rows and columns. This is computationally intensive for humans but conceptually useful.
